Frequently Asked Questions

What is the DPDPA compliance deadline for Indian companies?

The core obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) take effect on 13 May 2027. Organizations must ensure their consent architecture and data localization frameworks are operational before this date.

Why is cloud-based AI a liability for law firms?

Cloud AI routes privileged client data through third-party servers, breaching Attorney-Client Privilege. If the vendor goes bankrupt, the data can be trapped under IBC Section 14 moratoriums, leaving the law firm liable for a ₹250 Crore penalty.

What is the AI Graveyard?
