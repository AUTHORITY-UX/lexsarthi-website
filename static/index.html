Retrieval-first legal engine. Every answer is a direct quote from a statute, with the exact source file and page number. No generation. No summary. No hallucination.

01

No generation

The engine never writes prose. It retrieves verbatim text from an indexed corpus of Indian statutes, rules, and forms. What you read is what the Act says.

02

No hallucination

Every response carries the source filename, the page number, and a content hash. If the engine cannot find a match, it returns nothing rather than inventing a section.

03

No API cost

Runs on a local full-text index. No tokens, no rate limits, no per-query billing. Deploy it on your own infrastructure, offline, under your own control.
