Our Position

The company that built, trained, deployed, and profited from an AI system is accountable for what it does. The vocabulary that suggests otherwise — that the AI "decided," that the agent "went rogue," that the model "hallucinated" — is a liability shield. Our advisory work, our tools, and our research exist to remove that shield.
