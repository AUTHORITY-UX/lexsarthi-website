The AI Graveyard: Vendor Collapse Liability Framework

Fifteen casualties between 2022 and 2026, eight in 2026 alone. When your AI vendor fails, who answers for your data?

DIVERSE EXITS, IDENTICAL EXPOSURE

Court bankruptcy 33%, private wind-downs 47%, solvent tier-1 discontinuations 20%. Whatever the exit, your data, logic and privilege are exposed.

ABBREVIATED EXTRACTION WINDOWS

Median notice about 30 days, as short as 7 (Huxe). Without a pre-tested export protocol, loss is unrecoverable.

DATA ALIENATION VIA ASSET TRANSFER

Babylon to eMed, Olive to Waystar, Character.AI via a Section 363 sale: data passes to successors you never vetted.

CAPITAL IS NOT TENURE

Babylon $4.2B, Olive $4B, Builder.ai $1.5B peak valuations. Prestige and backers can hide brittle balance sheets.

NON-DELEGABLE DPDPA DUTY

Section 8(1): the Data Fiduciary stays responsible for processors. Section 8(5) safeguards and Section 15 liability continue through a vendor's liquidation. Fines attach to you.

INSOLVENCY FREEZES YOUR REMEDIES

IBC Section 14 moratoria can block termination and asset retrieval. Privileged work product may fall to liquidators without prior segregation.

The legal position: vendor distress never suspends your duties

DPDPA 2023

Accountability follows the Data Fiduciary, whatever the contract says.

Change of control

Transfer of data in a distressed sale is an unvetted processing event. Contracts need consent, audit rights and purge duties that survive insolvency.

IBC Section 14 moratorium

Ipso facto termination clauses may not hold. Cross-border administrations delay escrow retrieval.

Privilege in liquidation

Without documented segregation, privileged material sits within a liquidator's discretion.

Ten diligence inquiries for the board

  1. 1. TOTAL COST3-year cost of ownership, including price escalations?
  2. 2. COMMERCIAL VALUEWhich metric, which owner, which termination milestone?
  3. 3. SUPPLY CHAINWhich sub-tier models and clouds does the vendor depend on?
  4. 4. PORTABILITYPrompts, weights and embeddings exportable in 14 days?
  5. 5. DPDPA CONSENTNotices cover third-party processing; audit and verified deletion in contract?
  6. 6. DEFENSIBILITYCan you defend your oversight before a regulator or court?
  7. 7. PRIVILEGED ASSETSWhere are they processed, and are they shielded from liquidators?
  8. 8. ACCOUNTABILITYBoard-designated AI risk officer; shadow AI audited?
  9. 9. FALLBACKWhat restores operations by Monday if the vendor stops over a weekend?
  10. 10. SOVEREIGN INFRASTRUCTURECan sensitive workloads run on-premise or in an isolated VPC?

Conclusion: sovereign, local-first architecture

Open-weights models inside private infrastructure, with legal databases under direct institutional control, remove counterparty insolvency from your risk register. Local infrastructure needs security and maintenance, but it gives full jurisdictional sovereignty and continuity.
