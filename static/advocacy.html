Trust center

Built to be useful without becoming careless.

The system supports professional judgment; it does not replace it. Every public agent article carries its status and the next human decision.

Our operating promise

We design for least privilege, explicit uncertainty, traceable changes, and a clear handoff to a human professional. Sensitive work should have a purpose, an owner, and a deletion story.
