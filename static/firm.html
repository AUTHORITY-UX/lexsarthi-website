Our work covers corporate litigation, insolvency and restructuring, and domestic and international arbitration. We also advise on data protection and AI governance, ahead of the DPDPA compliance deadline on 13 May 2027.

Position

The human is the doer. The machine is the instrument. The company that built, trained, deployed, and profited from an AI system is accountable for what it does. The vocabulary that suggests otherwise — that the AI "decided," that the agent "went rogue," that the model "hallucinated" — is a liability shield. Our advisory work, our tools, and our research exist to remove that shield.

Research

We have authored a working paper titled Calculator, Not Mind: Anthropomorphic Language and the Risk of a Liability Shield for AI Developers. The paper argues that mental predicates applied to language models shift responsibility from the company to the tool. It is under review by legal practitioners before publication.
